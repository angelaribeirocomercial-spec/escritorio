## Story

**As a** banking-law attorney,  
**I want** a dedicated premium workspace to analyze banking contracts,  
**so that** I can review abusiveness signals, thesis fit, risk, and suggested requests from one structured screen.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A dedicated banking contract analysis route exists in the authenticated workspace.
2. The screen allows selecting a mock banking contract and shows a structured premium analysis tied to the selected document.
3. The analysis includes at least taxa de juros, CET, capitalizacao, tarifas, seguro embutido, comissao de permanencia, multa, clausulas sensiveis, indicios de abusividade, tese sugerida, risco processual, and pedidos sugeridos.
4. The screen exposes clear next actions aligned with LexIA and the broader product workflow.
5. The implementation remains compatible with the current tenant-aware architecture and mock data foundation.
