## Story

**As a** prospective buyer of LexIA Bancaria,  
**I want** a premium commercial landing page that explains the product clearly and convincingly,  
**so that** I can understand the value proposition and see the system as a serious SaaS offering.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The home page is replaced by a premium commercial landing page for LexIA Bancaria.
2. The landing includes hero, benefits, system mockup, LexIA block, modules, differentials, simulated social proof, FAQ, and final CTA.
3. The visual language matches the premium legal-tech tone of the authenticated product.
4. The landing exposes clear CTAs into the workspace and product demo flow.
