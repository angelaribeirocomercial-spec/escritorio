## Story

**As a** legal operations user evaluating ADVX against the reference suite,  
**I want** the workspace navigation and module pages to mirror the reference legal system more closely,  
**so that** the product feels familiar, lean, and operational while preserving Clara and the banking specialization.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The authenticated workspace navigation mirrors the reference suite order, excluding `SAC`.
2. `Pessoas`, `Equipe`, and `Agenda` expose the same primary sub-areas found in the reference suite.
3. `Financeiro`, `Relatórios`, `Estatísticas`, `Arquivos`, `Site`, and `Editor de texto` exist as authenticated modules in ADVX.
4. `Processos`, `Dashboard`, `Agenda`, and `Arquivos` are visually simplified to reduce demo-style density.
5. `Casos`, `Clientes`, `Tarefas`, and `Clara` stop acting as primary sidebar modules, while their routes continue to work.
6. The workspace supports `dark` and `light` theme modes.
7. Clara remains present as a contextual IA layer inside the suite rather than a competing primary module.
